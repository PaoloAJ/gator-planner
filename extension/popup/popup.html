GatorPlan Degree audit import

Before you start

Here's exactly what this extension does with your degree audit.

  • What it reads: only the degree audit that your ONE.UF degree audit page loads. It doesn't read other ONE.UF pages, your browsing, or your login.
  • What it removes: your name, UFID, grades, and GPA are deleted in your browser before anything is sent.
  • What it sends, and when: only when you click Open in GatorPlan, it sends your programs, requirements, and course history to GatorPlan. GatorPlan sends a summary to Anthropic's Claude to build your plan.
  • What's kept: nothing. The extension stores only that you agreed. GatorPlan doesn't save your audit. No ads, analytics, or selling of data.

GatorPlan is a student project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the University of Florida. Full privacy notice
